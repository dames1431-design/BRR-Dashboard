## The pedagogical aha

A child can decode every word in a sentence and still not hold what *happened* in it, because word recognition and meaning construction are different jobs; the six meaning categories show that "weak comprehension" is usually one specific category (here Event — what happened, then what, then why), so Tuesday's target is a 12-minute Event/Semantics lesson built on STQ prompts and a real book with a clear event chain, not more fluency drill and not "comprehension" in general.

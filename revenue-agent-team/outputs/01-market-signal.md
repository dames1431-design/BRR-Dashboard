# 01 — Market Signal: 14-minute BRR Dashboard walkthrough for K–5 literacy coaches

*Scout run 2026-11-15. Idea: a screen recording showing how a reading-risk score becomes tomorrow's Beyond Phonics lesson.*

## Verdict
**Moderate-to-strong signal.** Three real currents converge on this video: (1) 42 states + DC mandate evidence-based reading and K–3 screening, so coaches are drowning in screener data; (2) the best 2025 evidence (MIT survey) says most schools have no formal process to turn screening into intervention plans — the "score but no next step" gap is documented, not assumed; (3) 2026 research (SRI/The 74, Michigan State fade-out study) shows decoding gains stalling at comprehension — the Beyond Phonics lane. Caveat: the audience is budget-squeezed post-ESSER and wary of "one more dashboard," so sell the *method* first, software second. Audience in the brief holds; refinement — Georgia alone is funding ~1,300 new K–3 coaches for 2027-28, so "newly hired coach who needs a routine" beats "district director" as the primary persona.

## Red flags and compliance notes
- **Student data:** Demo with synthetic students only (FERPA). A lead magnet must never ask for student names, scores, or IDs.
- **Claims:** No "predicts dyslexia," "closes the gap," "grade-level by spring." MIT and Ohio sources frame screeners as flags needing further evaluation — adopt that framing.
- **Validity:** The BRR risk score has no published validation. Say "prototype," "decision-support," "research-informed." Do not call it an assessment.
- **Budget reality:** Districts are cutting tools, not adding; $3k–$25k licenses compete with free state curricula (MA) and $14/student screener bundles. Selling the *method* is lower friction than selling software.
- **Dashboard fatigue:** Coaches already have mCLASS, i-Ready, and Star dashboards. A fourth is a liability unless it ingests their existing scores.

## Handoff note to the Offer Architect
- **Lean into "score to Tuesday's lesson."** Open with a DIBELS/i-Ready composite everyone recognizes; end with a printed 12-minute domain mini-lesson plus STQ prompts. That is the gap the MIT survey quantifies.
- **Position Beyond Phonics as the comprehension layer, not a replacement.** The audience just bought phonics; 2026 SRI and fade-out research gives them permission to add meaning-level instruction without admitting error.
- **Make the multilingual-learner case explicit** (Ohio >80% flagged, MA English-only norm). "Reading risk or language-exposure flag?" is differentiated and timely.
- **Lead magnet is the method, not the tool:** a one-page "Six Meaning Categories x Five Domains" diagnostic grid, synthetic sample, no PII. Sell the $27–$97 playbook and $297+ cohort before the license.
- **Avoid** validation claims, outcome guarantees, and "replace your screener" language; frame BRR as decision-support on top of the data coaches already have.

## The pedagogical aha

A child can decode every word in a sentence and still not hold what *happened* in it, because word recognition and meaning construction are different jobs: the six meaning categories show that "weak comprehension" is usually one specific category (here, Event — the chain of what happened, then what, then why), so the Tuesday target is a 12-minute Event/Semantics lesson built on STQ prompts and a real book with a clear event chain, not another round of fluency drill and not "comprehension" in general.

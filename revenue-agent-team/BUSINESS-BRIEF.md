## Who is running this business
- An **education transformation leader and literacy researcher**.
- Author/owner of the **Beyond Phonics** framework. All pedagogy produced by this team is anchored
  in Beyond Phonics unless newer peer-reviewed research clearly enhances that baseline.
- Has built a working prototype: the **BRR Predictive Dashboard** (React). It scores a student's
  reading risk and recommends next-step instruction.

## What we have built (the asset)
The BRR Predictive Dashboard takes a student's attendance, work completion, time on task, fluency
(words correct per minute), comprehension, and two deeper layers:
- **Five language domains:** Phonology, Morphology, Syntax, Semantics, Pragmatics.
- **Six meaning categories (the "ontology"):** Object, Action, Property, State, Event, Relationship.

It returns a risk score (Low / Moderate / High), the weakest areas to focus on, and a ranked list
of recommended actions. The recommended actions use Beyond Phonics methods such as:
- STQ prompts tied to the day's read-aloud
- Orbit mapping (spiral review across the six categories)
- Domain mini-lessons (10–12 minutes)
- Real books at target Lexile ±75 with clear event chains
- Passcode Reasoning sheets → UCB bins (Systems/Processes, Cause/Motivation,
  Change/Development, Evidence/Reasoning) → one-sentence principle
- Caregiver scripts for High-risk students

## The problem they feel today
- Phonics-only programs plateau; students decode but do not comprehend.
- Risk shows up too late (end-of-year benchmarks) instead of early enough to act.
- Teachers get a score but not a *what-to-do-tomorrow* plan.
- Multilingual learners (e.g., Spanish–English) are flagged late or mis-flagged.

## Constraints and non-negotiables
- Research-grounded. No invented statistics. Cite real sources or say "estimate."
- Do not promise outcomes we cannot evidence ("guaranteed grade-level reading" is banned).
- Student data privacy matters (FERPA/COPPA awareness). Never suggest collecting PII in a lead magnet.

- Pedagogy anchor: Beyond Phonics first; newer research only if it strengthens the baseline.

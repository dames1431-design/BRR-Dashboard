# Disqualification-Pattern Tracking

Recurring compliance failures that remove otherwise strong proposals. Each row names where the pattern was seen so the checklist stays grounded.

| Pattern | Where seen | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Execution page not notarized or signed by wrong officer | CPS Spec 26-1355 Respondent's Execution Page (2026) | Execution-page fulfillment plan completed before signature; authorized representative = President and Managing Member |
| Insurance certificate missing a required endorsement or limit | general district RFPs | Pull insurance requirements into the gate checklist on day one; request broker quote at release, not at due date |
| References missing contact confirmation or wrong count | general | Reference letters drafted and signer-confirmed 10 days before due date |
| Portal submission uploaded after cutoff or in wrong format | Bonfire, IonWave, PASSPort portals | Upload a complete draft 48 hours early; final overwrite 24 hours early; screenshot confirmation |
| Pre-bid conference marked mandatory and missed | general | Mandatory gates are logged as the first due date, not the proposal date |
| Word-count or page limits exceeded on interrogatories | CPS Spec 26-1355 interrogatories (200-word limits) | Word counts recorded per question in the working draft |
| Pricing form altered or submitted outside the issuer's template | general | Pricing entered on the issuer form only; narrative pricing kept separate |
| MBE/WBE utilization plan or waiver request omitted | CPS, City of Chicago, Cook County | Diversity plan drafted from the solicitation's goal percentages at release |
| Legal entity mismatch between cover letter, W-9, SAM/UEI and insurance | three BRR entities | Legal-route decision recorded on the opportunity row before drafting begins |

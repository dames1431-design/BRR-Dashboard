# Commodity Codes to Search

Portals index poorly; searching by code catches items that keyword search misses. Confirm each state's own commodity scheme when adding a portal to the roster.

## NIGP (most district and state portals; eVA, eMMA, Texas ESBD, BidNet)
- 715-xx Publications and audiovisual materials: 715-24 Books, Curriculum and Instructional; 715-46 Library books; 715-60 Textbooks
- 785-xx School equipment, teaching aids and supplies: 785-05 Audio-visual/teaching aids; 785-90 Teaching aids, reading
- 918-xx Consulting services: 918-38 Education and training consulting; 918-68 Instructional consulting; 918-96 Training consulting
- 924-xx Educational services: 924-10 Curriculum development; 924-16 Instructor-led training; 924-20 In-service training; 924-36 Educational research; 924-40 Educational testing; 924-60 Reading and literacy instruction (where present); 924-85 Tutoring services
- 952-xx Human services: 952-86 Youth programs (after-school, OST)
- 961-xx Miscellaneous professional services: 961-45 Grant writing / program evaluation
- 208-xx / 209-xx Software (educational software, assessment platforms)

## State and district category codes to record per roster row
- Texas ESBD: Class/Item under NIGP; also TEA "Educational Services" categories.
- Illinois BidBuy: UNSPSC.
- Virginia eVA: NIGP.
- Maryland eMMA: NIGP.
- NYC PASSPort: NYC commodity codes plus industry classification (Human Services, Professional Services).
- CPS Bonfire: department categories (Teaching and Learning; Arts Education; Office of Language and Cultural Education; Office of Diverse Learner Supports; Office of Early Childhood).
- Michigan SIGMA: NIGP.
- Ohio Buys: UNSPSC.
